3. How and why we use your data

We process the personal data you submit on the basis of your consent (Section 6 of the DPDP Act), which you give by ticking the consent box on the contact form, and only for these purposes:

  • to reply to your enquiry and discuss the services you asked about;
  • to send you an automatic confirmation that we received your enquiry;
  • to prepare a proposal or quotation if you request one;
  • to keep a record of our communication and of your consent; and
  • to comply with legal obligations and protect the website from misuse.

We will not use your details for marketing newsletters, sell them, rent them, or share them with third parties for their own marketing.

6. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to respond and follow up, and for up to 24 months after our last communication, after which they are deleted. If you become a client, project-related records are kept for the period required by applicable laws (for example, tax and accounting laws). If you withdraw consent or ask us to erase your data, we delete it within a reasonable time unless the law requires us to keep it.

7. How we protect it

We follow reasonable security practices, including: encrypted HTTPS connections with HTTP Strict Transport Security; a strict Content Security Policy and other security headers; spam protection on the form; storing enquiries in a private account accessible only to authorised team members; and limiting access to people who need it to respond to you. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your data and will act promptly, including notifying you and the Data Protection Board of India where required, if a personal data breach occurs.

8. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you have the right to:

  • Access a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how it is processed (Section 11);
  • Correct, complete, update or erase your personal data (Section 12);
  • Withdraw your consent at any time, as easily as you gave it (Section 6(4)). Withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out before it;
  • Grievance redressal through our Grievance Officer (Section 13); and
  • Nominate another person to exercise these rights in the event of your death or incapacity (Section 14).

To exercise any of these rights, email our Grievance Officer (below) from the email address you used, or mention it in a new enquiry. We may ask you to confirm your identity before acting on a request.
